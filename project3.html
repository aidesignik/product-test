Analytics Dashboard Redesign

Making complex data accessible to every stakeholder

Overview

An enterprise analytics platform had powerful data under the hood but an interface that only data analysts could navigate. This redesign made the same data accessible to product managers, executives, and support leads — without dumbing anything down.

The Challenge

The existing dashboard was built by engineers for engineers. It surfaced raw data in dense tables with minimal visualization. Non-technical stakeholders relied on analysts to pull reports, creating bottlenecks and delays in decision-making.

The Approach

I conducted stakeholder interviews across five departments to map their unique data needs. The redesign introduced role-based views, interactive charts with drill-down capabilities, and a natural-language search for quick data lookups. Every design decision was validated through usability testing with real users from each department.

The Outcome

Stakeholder adoption increased by 60% in the first quarter. Self-serve data lookups replaced 70% of ad-hoc analyst requests. The platform received an internal NPS score of 72, up from 31 pre-redesign. Placeholder for additional details.
