AI-Assisted Submissions

Helping non-technical users complete complex forms without friction

Overview

Regulatory submission forms are notoriously complex — full of jargon, conditional logic, and strict formatting requirements. This project introduced an AI assistant that guides non-technical users through the process, reducing errors and dramatically improving completion rates.

The Challenge

Users were abandoning submissions mid-way due to confusing form fields and unclear requirements. Support teams were overwhelmed with tickets asking for help with basic form completion. The existing interface assumed domain expertise that most users simply didn't have.

The Approach

I designed a conversational layer on top of the existing form infrastructure. The AI assistant breaks complex fields into plain-language questions, auto-suggests values based on context, and validates entries in real time. The interface adapts its level of guidance based on user behavior — offering more help to those who need it while staying out of the way for experienced users.

The Outcome

Form completion rates improved by 35%. Support tickets related to form help dropped by 50%. The assistant was particularly effective for first-time submitters, where completion rates improved by over 60%. Placeholder for additional metrics and testimonials.
