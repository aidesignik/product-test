Mentoring

Are you a junior designer trying to break into the industry, or in your first year or two and looking for thoughtful feedback? I occasionally mentor designers and am happy to help when I can.

How I can help

Portfolio feedback — improving how you present your work, structure case studies, and clearly show your impact.

Breaking in — applications, interviews, and how to position yourself with limited experience.

Career questions — choosing a direction, evaluating offers, and growing in your first few years.

How to reach out

Send me a link to your portfolio (or what you're working on) and one specific thing you'd like feedback on. The more focused your question, the more useful I can be.

No charge — I do this alongside a full-time role, so I can't respond to every request, but I'll do my best.
