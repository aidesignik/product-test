Smarter Feedback Collection

AI-driven insights for enterprise teams

Overview

Enterprise teams collect thousands of feedback responses each quarter, but most of that data goes unanalyzed. This project reimagined how product teams collect, process, and act on user feedback by integrating AI-powered analysis into the existing workflow.

The Challenge

The existing feedback system was fragmented — responses lived in spreadsheets, survey tools, and support tickets. Product managers spent weeks manually tagging and categorizing feedback before they could identify patterns. By the time insights surfaced, they were often outdated.

The Approach

I led the design of a unified feedback hub that automatically ingests responses from multiple sources, uses natural language processing to cluster themes, and surfaces prioritized insights in real time. The design focused on progressive disclosure — keeping the interface simple for quick scanning while enabling deep dives for thorough analysis.

The Outcome
